GAME SETTINGS

SAVE SETTINGS ON saves your settings automatically. OFF resets settings to defaults now and on future launches; your display name is saved separately. BACKGROUND TERRAIN ON: full terrain scenery. OFF: hides distant mountains, trees, volcano details and detailed shorelines for better performance while keeping simplified ground and water indicators. WASD MODE: W/S SPEED, A/D TURN. MOUSE MODE: cursor steers aircraft at 2× turn rate; WASD/arrow movement inputs are ignored. HELICARRIER follows the cursor in all four directions. DESTROYER moves left/right; cursor up/down aims its guns. ZOOM-OUT RANGE: lower values allow less zooming out; higher values allow more, up to the reduced maximum distance. F/SPACE FIRE, X MISSILE, G FLARES, SHIFT BOOST, O OPENS SETTINGS.

Tutorial

FLY & AIM
Use W/S to change speed and A/D to turn. Mouse controls can be enabled in Settings. Hold SHIFT for WEP boost; your available boost recharges over time.
MISSILE LOCK
Click an enemy aircraft or air defense inside the red seeker cone. Keep it in the cone until the lock completes, then press X to fire. Closer targets lock faster.
FLARES & WARNINGS
Press G to release flares and try to decoy incoming missiles. “MISSILE LOCK” means an enemy is acquiring you; “MISSILE INCOMING” means a missile is tracking you. Move, turn, and use flares together.
F / SPACE: FIRE GUN · X: MISSILE · R: START RELOADING ALL WEAPON SLOTS · P: PAUSE · O: SETTINGS · CLICK A TARGET: LOCK
